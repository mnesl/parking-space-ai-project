# AeroPark Smart Garage — General Information, Location & Rules

## 1. Facility Overview
Welcome to **AeroPark Smart Garage**, a multi-level automated parking facility located in the heart of the Downtown Tech District. Our garage offers 24/7 security monitoring, license plate recognition (LPR) at entry and exit gates, covered weather-protected stalls, and high-speed Level 2 electric vehicle (EV) charging stations.

## 2. Location & Directions
- **Physical Address:** 450 Innovation Boulevard, Downtown Tech District, Gate B.
- **GPS Coordinates:** `41.0082° N, 28.9784° E`
- **How to Enter:** Approach from Innovation Boulevard heading northbound. Turn right at the blue "AeroPark Automated Entry" sign immediately after the Metro Central Plaza intersection.
- **Pedestrian Access:** Elevators and stairwells connect Levels P1, P2, and P3 directly to the Metro Central Skybridge and Main Lobby.

## 3. Parking Zones & Vehicle Height Limits
Our garage is divided into three specialized zones:
1. **Zone A (Standard Covered Parking - Levels P1 & P2):**
   - Designed for standard sedans, hatchbacks, and compact SUVs.
   - Maximum vehicle height clearance: **2.10 meters (6 ft 10 in)**.
2. **Zone B (EV Charging & Green Zone - Level P1 East):**
   - Equipped with 22 kW Level 2 AC chargers (Type 2 / J1772 connectors).
   - Customers parking in Zone B must actively plug in their vehicle.
3. **Zone C (VIP & Oversized / Executive Van Zone - Ground Level):**
   - Extra-wide spaces located directly next to the main security desk and elevator lobby.
   - Maximum vehicle height clearance: **2.65 meters (8 ft 8 in)**.

## 4. Safety & Facility Rules
- Maximum speed limit inside the garage is **10 km/h (6 mph)**.
- Smoking, open flames, and vehicle maintenance/repairs are strictly prohibited inside the facility.
- Roof racks and cargo boxes must be accounted for before entering Zone A.
- Overnight parking is permitted for registered reservations up to 14 consecutive days.
